# nitishdotdev robots policy
#
# Allow search and retrieval. Restrict the dedicated training crawlers listed
# below and allow Google-Extended for its documented training and grounding uses.
#
# robots.txt controls crawling, not what a system does with content after it has
# been fetched. Search engines and AI systems may ignore or interpret these rules
# differently. Permissions here do not guarantee rankings, citations or answers.
#
# Crawler purposes were checked against each operator's documentation:
#   OpenAI:    https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots
#   Anthropic: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8896518-anthropic-crawlers
#   Apple:     https://support.apple.com/en-ae/119829
#   Google:    Google-Extended documentation (Google's common crawlers list)
#
# Explicit decisions:
#   Training crawlers, disallowed: GPTBot (OpenAI foundation-model training),
#     ClaudeBot (Anthropic training collection), Applebot-Extended (Apple
#     Intelligence training; does not affect Siri or Spotlight).
#   Google-Extended: allowed. It governs Gemini training AND grounding in
#     Gemini Apps and Vertex AI, and the two cannot be separated. Disallowing it
#     would also remove this site from Gemini retrieval. It has no effect on
#     Google Search or AI Overviews, which use the Search Console generative AI
#     control instead. This is a deliberate owner decision; see README.
#   Search and retrieval crawlers: allowed.
